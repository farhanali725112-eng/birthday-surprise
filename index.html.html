Aaj ka din bohat special hai,

kyun ke aaj Maryam ka birthday hai. 🎂✨




Dil se dua hai ke tumhari zindagi

hamesha phoolon ki tarah khoobsurat,

sitaron ki tarah roshan aur

khushiyon se bhari rahe. 🌷💫


“Tumhari har muskurahat ek khoobsurat

phool ki tarah hai — hamesha yunhi

khilti rehna. 🌸💗”


Allah tumhari har achi dua qabool kare,

tumhare har khwab ko khoobsurat haqeeqat

banaye aur tumhe hamesha khush rakhe. 🤍✨




Keep smiling, keep shining,

and always stay this beautiful soul. 💕
